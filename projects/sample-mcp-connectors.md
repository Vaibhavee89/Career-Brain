## What it is

A set of reusable MCP connectors and role-based access control for the internal AI chat platform at Scopely, so teams could use AI tools with access limited to what they need.

## Why I built it

Teams needed standardized LLM access to Google Workspace, Confluence, Unity, internal APIs, and semantic search. Source: CV, Scopely AI Engineering Intern entry.

## Key decisions (and why)

- Built 15+ reusable MCP connectors that work across Claude Code, Codex, and the internal AI platform, rather than one-off integrations per tool. Source: CV.
- Added role-based access control (RBAC) for MCPs so each team gets team-specific access. Source: CV.

## Results / metrics

- Serves 3,000+ employees on the internal AI chat platform. Source: CV.
